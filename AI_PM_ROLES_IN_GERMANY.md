# 🇩🇪 AI PM roles in Germany

Looking for an AI product role without leaving Berlin? This list brings together local openings and remote jobs available to people living in Germany.

Last checked: **11 November 2026**. There are **106 roles across 57 companies**.

Curated by Gleb Melnikov: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/melnikovgleb/) · [X/Twitter](https://x.com/hleb_gleb_)

Each role must be open on the employer's careers site and list Berlin or a remote arrangement that covers Germany, Europe/EMEA, or the world. Employers must be beyond pre-seed, or established bootstrapped, corporate-backed, or public companies. Roles that require relocation are excluded.

Employers can be based anywhere. Public companies, investors, and services firms are included when they hire for product roles. The [Berlin directory](README.md) has a narrower scope, so some employers appear only here.

Jobs close between updates, and this list will miss some openings. Before applying, check language, time-zone, and attendance requirements. Europe/EMEA/global remote roles without an explicit Germany location are marked “Germany hiring not explicitly confirmed”. Country-limited remote roles outside Germany are excluded. Confirm employment arrangements and work authorization before applying.

💰 Salary ranges come from the employer's job description. If no range is published, none is shown here.

Each entry shows 👥 team size · 💰 disclosed funding or ownership · 💼 company-wide openings. Opening counts were refreshed on 11 November 2026 where a complete official feed was available; other counts remain from the previous review. Team sizes and funding come from earlier disclosures and were not rechecked in full. `~openings` means a reliable count wasn't available.

Validation: reviewed company sites and careers sources for **305 employers**, including all 245 companies from the previous Berlin directory. 115 employers exposed a structured job feed. An accessible homepage alone is not evidence of an empty job board; unresolved checks remain visible below. This is a manual snapshot, not a scheduled alert service.

Parloa’s live AMP role was recovered on Ashby. Previous ClickHouse Security and IDnow Trust Platform Modules listings are absent from their current feeds. Sharpist, Veeva, and Nodes & Links listings were removed from the active count pending verification, not declared closed. RIB Software’s previously unconfirmed role remains excluded.
