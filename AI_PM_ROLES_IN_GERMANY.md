# 🇩🇪 AI PM roles in Germany

The full directory was checked on **20 August 2026**; the DataBerlin comparison and newest additions were checked on **25 August 2026**. This is the repository's single tracker for current AI/ML Product Manager work that can be done while living in Berlin or elsewhere in Germany: local and hybrid roles, Germany-remote roles, Europe/EMEA-remote roles, and genuinely global-remote roles. The employer may be headquartered anywhere.

**Curated by Gleb Melnikov:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/melnikovgleb/) · [X/Twitter](https://x.com/hleb_gleb_)

The list currently contains **137 roles across 91 companies**. Inclusion requires a live role on the employer's own careers site, an explicit Berlin/Germany/Europe/EMEA/global location signal, and an employer that is beyond pre-seed or is an established bootstrapped, corporate-backed, or public company. Roles that require moving away from Berlin are excluded.

This is a high-confidence live snapshot rather than a mathematically exhaustive list: international job boards change continuously, and some employers do not publish every eligible hiring country. Follow the application link to confirm that a role remains open and to check payroll-entity, work-authorization, language, time-zone, and remote-work requirements.

[DataBerlin's Product Manager page](https://databerlin.net/jobs/category/product-manager) is used as a discovery cross-check, not as proof that a vacancy is live. On 25 August it contained 40 unique roles at 32 employers after removing one duplicated Cortea listing; all 32 employers also appeared in the [DataBerlin company directory](https://databerlin.net/companies). This tracker includes 31 of those roles; nine data or growth PM jobs without a direct AI/ML product remit were excluded. Every included vacancy was then checked on the employer's own careers site.

💰 A role-level compensation range is shown only when an explicit range appears in the employer-hosted job description. If the description does not state a range, no compensation estimate is shown.

**Entry metadata:** 👥 team size · 💰 latest disclosed financing or ownership status · 💼 current company openings. A `~` means the linked board does not expose a reliable count.
