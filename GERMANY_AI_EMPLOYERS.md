# 🇩🇪 AI employers that can hire in Germany

A Germany-wide directory for finding AI product work without limiting the search to startups or Berlin-headquartered companies. It combines **901 active Germany-headquartered AI companies** with **185 additional employers** that have a German hiring path.

The application-ready section contains **32 currently advertised AI or AI-company product roles** whose posting allows Berlin, Germany-remote work, or a Berlin-compatible hybrid setup. Roles were checked on **29 July 2026**.

**Curated by Gleb Melnikov:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/melnikovgleb/) · [X/Twitter](https://x.com/hleb_gleb_)

For the Berlin startup directory, see [README.md](README.md). For the broader Berlin product-role tracker, see [PM_OPENINGS.md](PM_OPENINGS.md).

> **Scope and confidence.** The Germany-headquartered index is the complete live [appliedAI German AI Startup Landscape](https://www.appliedai-institute.de/ressourcen/ai-startups-deutschland/) after collapsing duplicate company websites: 933 source records become 901 unique companies. appliedAI screens for active companies, German headquarters, substantive AI, at least two FTEs, and a 2015-or-later founding or AI pivot. It does not publish financing stage per record, so this discovery index can include pre-seed companies. The additional-employer section is a curated hiring universe, not a mathematical claim that every company worldwide with occasional German hiring has been captured. Always confirm the location and employment terms in the live posting.
