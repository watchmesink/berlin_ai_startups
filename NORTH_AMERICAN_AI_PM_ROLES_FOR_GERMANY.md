# 🌎 North American AI PM roles open to Germany-based candidates

Checked through **23 August 2026**. This focused list covers current AI/ML Product Manager roles at employers headquartered or co-headquartered in the United States or Canada where the employer's own posting explicitly supports Berlin, Germany, Europe/EMEA, or genuinely global remote work. Every role can be pursued without relocating away from Germany, subject to the employer's payroll, work-authorization, language, and time-zone requirements.

**Curated by Gleb Melnikov:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/melnikovgleb/) · [X/Twitter](https://x.com/hleb_gleb_)

The list contains **33 roles across 25 companies** and is a focused slice of the complete [AI PM roles in Germany](AI_PM_ROLES_IN_GERMANY.md) tracker.

Discovery sources included [Lenny's Jobs](https://lenny.trueup.io/), [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs), [Y Combinator Jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs), [Remotive](https://remotive.com/remote-jobs/product), and [AI Startup Jobs](https://www.aistartupjobs.com/). These boards were used to find leads only; inclusion requires a live role on the employer's own careers site. Aggregator entries that had expired, were restricted to North America, or used “remote” without confirming German/European eligibility were excluded.

💰 A role-level compensation range is shown only when the employer-hosted description publishes one. US-only ranges are labelled as such and should not be treated as German offers.

**Entry metadata:** 👥 team size · 💰 latest disclosed financing or ownership status · 💼 current company openings. A `~` means the linked board does not expose a reliable count.
